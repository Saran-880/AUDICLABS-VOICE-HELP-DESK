What it does
Noise Suppressor
noise-suppressor
Available
Remove background noise from speech: one file, up to 25 per call, or live audio in real time.
Text to Speech
tts
Available
Natural speech from text with English, Arabic and Indian English voices, returned as MP3.
Voice Conversion
voice-conversion
On request
Re-voice a recording in another speaker's voice while keeping the words, timing and emotion.
Speech to Text
stt
Available
Transcribe recordings in 40 languages, including English, Arabic and Hindi, with automatic language detection.
Speaker Recognition
speaker-recognition
On request
Enrol a voice once, then verify or identify the speaker from new audio.
Speaker Diarization
diarization
Available
Split multi-speaker audio into labelled turns: who spoke, and when. Up to 8 speakers, any length.
